In standard tuning the strings are **E A D G B E**, from the thickest (low E, on the left) to the thinnest (high E, on the right). The top row shows each open string. Each row below it is one fret higher, and each fret raises the note by a half step (a semitone).

- **Filled circles** are the natural notes: A B C D E F G.
- **Dark circles** are sharps and flats. Each has two names for the same sound, so A♯ and B♭ are the same note.
- There is no sharp or flat between **B and C** or between **E and F**. Those pairs are only one fret apart.
- At the **12th fret** the notes match the open strings again, one octave higher, and the pattern repeats.

**Find a note:** tap a note, such as D, to see everywhere it appears on the neck. Every other note fades out, and each D is coloured by its octave. Notes with the same colour (and the same small number) are the exact same pitch, played in different places. A different colour means the same note name, higher or lower.
