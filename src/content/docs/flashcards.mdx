Build a deck from the chords below, then press **Start**. The metronome counts in 4/4, with a higher click on beat 1. The card switches to the next chord on every beat 1, so change chords right on the one. Use **−** and **+** or the slider to change the tempo.

Keep an eye on **Next** so you can get your fingers ready before the change.
