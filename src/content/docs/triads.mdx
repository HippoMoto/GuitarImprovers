A **triad** is the three notes that make a chord. A full chord just repeats some of those notes on more strings, so you can play only the triad and still get the same chord.

Each row shows a major chord on the left, with its three triad notes lit up. On the right is the triad on its own: three notes on the **D, G and B strings**, all at the same fret.

**It's the same shape in every row.** To get to the next chord, keep your fingers in the shape and slide it along the neck: usually 2 frets, but only 1 fret from B to C and from E to F.
