Use the filters to narrow the list. Tap any mix of **Major**, **Minor** and **7th** (like A7 or D7), and one or more root notes. For example, select E, G and A to see only the E, G and A chords. Sharp and flat chords (like F♯ or B♭) are hidden until you turn on **Sharps & flats**.

**How to read a chord diagram:** the vertical lines are the strings, with the low E string on the left and the high e string on the right. The horizontal lines are frets.

- **○** above a string: play it open (no fingers).
- **×** above a string: don't play that string.
- **Numbers** in the dots are fingers: 1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky.
- **A long bar** is a barre: lay one finger flat across all those strings.
- **"3fr"** beside the diagram: the top fret shown is the 3rd fret. With no label, the thick line at the top is the nut.
